## What you don't get

These are unmanaged KVM VPS. RackNerd handles the platform; the operating system, the stack, updates, backups and security are yours. Support answers platform questions around the clock, not "please fix my WordPress" questions.

Other limits worth knowing before you pay:

- **One IPv4 address per plan.** No IPv6 is listed on the specials.
- **Custom ISO installs require a support ticket**, though ctrl can reinstall a stock OS at any time.
- **No refunds and no money-back guarantee.** RackNerd's knowledgebase states that per their terms of service they do not offer refunds or a money back guarantee. Confirm the datacenter and the plan size before you order, because there is no cooling-off window.
- **2026 Black Friday details do not exist yet.** No plans, prices, location count or stock levels have been announced.

This is a poor fit if you want a managed server, a point-and-click site builder, or a written SLA. A control-panel hosting account is the right product for that, not a root-access VPS.
